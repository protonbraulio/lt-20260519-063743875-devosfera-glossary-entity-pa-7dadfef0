## Definition

Local search visibility refers to the prominence and relevance of a business or organization in local search engine results. It's a critical aspect of local SEO, focusing on helping potential customers find your business online when they're searching for relevant products or services in their vicinity.

Key terms include local SEO, local pack, local knowledge graph, and local citations. Alternate names might be local online visibility or local search presence.

## Related terms

Understanding local search visibility also involves familiarizing oneself with related terms such as:

- **Local SEO**: The practice of optimizing a business's online presence to attract more local customers through relevant searches.

- **Local Pack**: The map and list of local businesses that appear in Google's search results for local queries.

- **Local Knowledge Graph**: The information box that appears on the right side of Google's search results, providing details about a business, such as its address, phone number, and opening hours.

- **Local Citations**: Mentions of your business's name, address, and phone number (NAP) on other websites, which help improve your local search visibility.
