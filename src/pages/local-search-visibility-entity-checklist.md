## Validation checks

To validate the local search visibility implementation and ensure its internal consistency, perform the following checks:

Verify that your NAP is consistent across all platforms, including your website, Google My Business, and local directories. Inconsistencies can negatively impact your local search rankings.

Check that your website's on-page SEO is optimized for local search. This includes incorporating local keywords, having a mobile-friendly design, and ensuring fast loading speeds.

Confirm that your local citations are accurate and up-to-date. Remove any duplicate listings or incorrect information that could harm your local search visibility.

Evaluate your website's local link profile. Ensure that you have a healthy mix of local, relevant, and authoritative backlinks. This can help improve your website's local search rankings and credibility.

Assess your local search rankings for your target keywords. Use tools like Google Search Console or BrightLocal to track your progress and identify areas for improvement.
