## How to connect definitions without overlap

Connecting definitions without overlap involves ensuring that your entity coverage is distinct, relevant, and adds value to users and search engines. Avoid duplicating content or confusing users with overlapping definitions.

Start by clearly defining each entity and topic. Use unique, descriptive titles and meta descriptions for each page or section. Ensure that the content is focused, informative, and addresses user intent.

Use internal linking to connect related entities and topics. This helps search engines understand the context and relevance of your content, and improves user navigation and engagement.

Regularly audit and update your entity coverage to ensure it remains relevant, accurate, and free of overlap. Remove or merge duplicate content, and update definitions as needed to reflect changes in your business or industry.
