## Core entities behind Local Search Visibility

Local search visibility is underpinned by several core entities. These entities are the building blocks that enable businesses to appear prominently in local search results. They include the business's name, address, and phone number (NAP), business category, and business hours.

Understanding these core entities is crucial for sales teams qualifying higher-intent leads. By ensuring these entities are accurate and consistent, teams can improve the business's local search visibility, driving more relevant traffic to the website and physical location.

## How those entities connect to Local Search Visibility

The core entities connect to local search visibility through various means. Accurate and consistent NAP information ensures that search engines can correctly identify and display the business in local search results. This is particularly important for businesses with multiple locations.

Business category plays a significant role in local search visibility. A well-defined and accurate category helps search engines understand what the business does and who its target audience is. This can significantly improve the business's visibility in local search results.

Business hours are another critical entity. Accurate business hours ensure that customers can find up-to-date information about when the business is open. This can significantly improve customer satisfaction and drive more foot traffic to the business.

## Examples of entity framing for sales teams qualifying higher-intent leads

When qualifying higher-intent leads, sales teams should frame the conversation around these core entities. For example, they might ask the lead about their current NAP information and how it's displayed across various platforms. This can help the team identify any inconsistencies or inaccuracies that could be hindering the business's local search visibility.

Similarly, the team might ask about the business's category and business hours. This can help them understand the business's target audience and how they operate. This information can then be used to tailor the sales pitch and demonstrate how improved local search visibility can drive more relevant traffic to the business.

## Definitions worth clarifying next

While the core entities are crucial, there are several other definitions worth clarifying next. These include citation sources, on-page optimization, and local link building. Each of these plays a role in improving local search visibility and should be understood by sales teams qualifying higher-intent leads.

Citation sources are platforms where the business's NAP information is listed. Ensuring these citations are accurate and consistent can significantly improve local search visibility. On-page optimization involves optimizing the business's website to improve its visibility in local search results. Local link building involves building links from local, relevant websites to the business's website. Each of these can significantly improve local search visibility.
